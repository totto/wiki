# Agent Pilsen & SDD Oslo

*April 16, 2026 · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7450647134846107648)*

**57 reactions · 9 comments · 2,972 views**

---

We've been running Agent Pilsen for a while now.        
                                                   
 Three events in. Small group. Developers. Cold beers. Thursdays at Eileff Landhandleri.  
                                                   
 The agentic space is moving at a pace where last week's mental model is already outdated.     
 Nobody can keep up alone. What works, what doesn't, what just shipped, what just broke — the volume of change is relentless.

That's exactly why informal peer sharing matters more right now than it ever has. Not a newsletter. Not a keynote. Just sitting across from someone who's been in the weeds, comparing notes.

No fixed agenda. No presentations. Just "what are you building, what did you learn, what surprised you?" — and enough cold beers to be honest about it.

We borrowed the spirit from JavaPilsen: keep it small, keep it informal, keep it real. Three events in, the conversations are already different than anything you get from a conference or a Slack channel.

If you're building with agents in Oslo and want to join — reach out. Or just show up Thursday at 17:00.

Welcome to the dark side.
