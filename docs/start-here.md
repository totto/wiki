# Start Here

This site is one long argument, told across ~175 posts and four deep guides: **the way you get value from AI is by building infrastructure and method around it — not by prompting harder.** Encoded knowledge compounds; improvisation doesn't. And an agent you can't *defend* is a liability, not an asset.

That thesis has two faces. [**Skill-Driven Development**](/topics/skill-driven-development/) is how a *person or team* compounds capability with AI. [**Defendable Agents**](/topics/defendable-agents/) is how an *agent's decisions* become reproducible and evidenced. The [**Knowledge Context Protocol**](/topics/knowledge-context-protocol/) is the substrate underneath both, and [**the agentic web**](/topics/agentic-web/) is the world they're built for. *Compound and defendable are the same infrastructure seen from two angles.*

Pick the lane that fits what you're trying to do.
